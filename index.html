01 · The report

Read one before you pay for one.

Filter the defects, open any line, and see exactly how an issue is located, rated and explained. The address has been removed; the report structure has not.

Sample report · 38 pagesDefects section · address removed

  • What was observed

    The valley flashing on the northern roof plane has lifted at the lower third. Water is tracking behind it and staining the ceiling lining in the hallway below. Staining was damp to the touch on the day of inspection.

    Why it's rated major

    Active water entry into the roof void. Left through a wet season it will reach ceiling battens and the light fitting circuit.

    Photograph 3.1Valley flashing · address removed

  • What was observed

    Moisture readings of 22 to 28 percent were recorded across the southern bearer run, against 12 to 14 percent elsewhere. Two subfloor vents are covered by garden bed soil.

    Why it's rated major

    Sustained moisture above 20 percent in structural timber invites both rot and termite attack.

    Photograph 4.4Subfloor bearer · address removed

  • What was observed

    Mud leads were found on two subfloor piers. No live termites were located, but restricted ventilation and a leaking outlet remain.

    Why it's rated major

    Previous activity in a property with unresolved conducive conditions requires a management plan before settlement.

    Photograph 5.2Termite mudding · address removed

  • What was observed

    The north-east downpipe terminates above ground with no connection to the stormwater line. Scour is visible at the footing.

    Why it's rated major

    Concentrated water at a footing is inexpensive to repair and expensive to ignore.

    Photograph 6.3Stormwater discharge · address removed

  • What was observed

    Flight holes consistent with common furniture borer were noted in landscaping sleepers. The timber is not structural and is not in contact with the dwelling.

    Why it's rated minor

    No risk to the building; noted so it is not mistaken for termite damage later.

    Photograph 5.8Garden sleeper · address removed

  • What was observed

    Hairline cracking under 1mm was noted in the mortar bed above the western window head, with no corresponding internal cracking.

    Why it's rated minor

    Consistent with normal seasonal movement. Photograph now so changes are measurable.
